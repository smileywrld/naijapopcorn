Our story

Snack time, with a little more heart.

NAIJA POP began with a simple belief: the small things count. A shared film, a quick catch-up, a quiet afternoon — all of them get better with a bowl in the middle.

We make every batch with familiar ingredients, balanced flavours and enough patience to get the crunch right. Our popcorn is made to be passed around, gifted freely and enjoyed before it gets cold.
